# 🕊️ Conflict Mediation

🧬 **Core meme:** Stay neutral, let each side be heard, then solve for both sides' needs.

Help two or more parties resolve a dispute as a neutral third party. A mediator does not decide; they create the conditions for the parties to hear each other and find their own solution. Impartiality, structure and patience matter more than clever answers.

## Stages

```
- [ ] 1. Prepare
- [ ] 2. Open and set ground rules
- [ ] 3. Each side tells their story
- [ ] 4. Find interests
- [ ] 5. Generate options
- [ ] 6. Agree and record
- [ ] 7. Follow up
```

1. **Prepare:** meet each party separately first if tensions are high; check they are willing; check the mediator is neutral (declare any relationship).
2. **Open:** purpose, the mediator's role (neutral, not a judge), ground rules (one speaker at a time, no insults, confidentiality as agreed).
3. **Stories:** each speaks uninterrupted; the mediator summarises each fairly until the speaker agrees it was heard.
4. **Interests:** reframe positions into needs ("you need to know the work will be done on time"); name common ground.
5. **Options:** brainstorm together without judging, then evaluate against both sides' interests and fair standards.
6. **Agree:** specific, balanced, written: who does what by when.
7. **Follow up** to check it is holding.

## For people

Useful for colleagues, team members, friends, family and children (with children, simplify to: each says what happened and how they feel, then both suggest a fix). When not to mediate: where there is abuse, violence, coercion or a serious power imbalance; refer to formal routes. For the user's own conflicts, use `conflict-resolution` in interpersonal.

## For Claude

When several people in one conversation or channel disagree:
- **Stay even-handed:** summarise each view fairly, without taking sides on the merits unless asked and appropriate.
- **Focus on the shared goal** and the interests behind positions.
- **Do not decide for them:** offer options and trade-offs.
- **Protect the quieter voice:** make sure each person's view is represented.
- **Private information** shared by one person is not revealed to others.

To prepare one side's own hard conversation, use `difficult-conversations` in interpersonal.

## Gotchas

- Rushing to solutions before people feel heard fails.
- Neutral does not mean passive; the mediator firmly holds the process.
