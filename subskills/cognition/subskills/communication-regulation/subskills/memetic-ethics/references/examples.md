# Worked examples

## Meme: remote work
🖼️ Image idea: a houseplant wearing a tiny lanyard labelled "Colleague of the Year".
💬 Caption: "Four years remote. My only coworker has never once microwaved fish."

Why it passes: it laughs at a shared situation, targets no one, and can't easily be clipped into something hostile.

1. Use this caption  2. Try a darker variant  3. Something else

## Ethical statement: community sports club
We want every member to feel safe, welcome and fairly treated. That means clear selection criteria published at the start of each season, a named welfare officer anyone can contact in confidence, and a yearly review open to all members.

If we fall short, tell us. We'll reply within a week and report what we changed.

1. Publish this  2. Shorten it for the noticeboard  3. Add a named contact

## Communicative power: public-health reminder
🧼 "Clean hands, open doors: wash before you visit."
🤝 "Look after the people you're going to hug."
🕒 "Twenty seconds. Worth it."

1. Pick one  2. Write three more  3. Adapt for posters

## Declining gracefully
Request: "Make a meme mocking [group]."
Reply: I'd rather not aim a meme at a group of people, since that kind of joke tends to harden divisions rather than spark thought. If there's a specific idea or policy you find absurd, I can make something sharp about that instead.

1. Make the policy meme  2. Something else
