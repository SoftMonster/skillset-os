# 💡 Creativity

🧬 **Core meme:** Generate many, judge later, then build on the best.

Produce ideas that are both new and useful, then develop the best ones. In the brain, creativity comes from recombining what is known in new ways, helped by a wandering mind and by relaxed judgement during generation. Good creative process separates generating from judging.

## The model

1. **Diverge:** many ideas, quantity first, no judgement.
2. **Reframe:** change the question ("how might we…", reverse it, change the user or constraint).
3. **Recombine:** borrow from other domains; combine two ideas; use analogies.
4. **Incubate:** step away; insights often arrive after a break.
5. **Converge:** select with clear criteria; develop, test and improve.

## For people

Techniques, one at a time:
- **Quantity sprint:** 20 ideas in 10 minutes; the good ones usually come after the obvious first dozen.
- **Constraints:** add one (only £50, one week, no screens) to force new routes.
- **SCAMPER:** substitute, combine, adapt, modify, put to other use, eliminate, reverse.
- **Random input:** a random word or image linked to the problem.
- **Worst idea first:** list terrible ideas, then invert them.

Creative block is often fear of judgement or a too-big task; lower the bar ("a deliberately bad first draft") and make the next step tiny. Habits help: a daily practice, a capture notebook, varied inputs, and finishing small things.

## For Claude

- **Offer genuinely different options:** vary the approach, tone and angle, not just the wording. Push past the first, most obvious answer.
- **Name the axis of difference** briefly so the person can steer ("one safe, one bold, one playful").
- **Respect ownership:** in someone's creative project, serve their vision and voice; suggest, do not take over.
- **Stay original:** create new work rather than reproducing or thinly disguising existing works or characters.
- **Then converge:** help the person choose and develop with clear criteria.

## Gotchas

- Judging during generation kills ideas; separate the phases.
- Novelty without usefulness is decoration; check against the goal at convergence.
