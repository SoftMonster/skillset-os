# Safety governance

A nested skillset. Pick the member whose "Use when" matches the request and read its instructions; for a nested skillset or a zip, run `python3 <top>/scripts/skillset.py open <path>` to get the file to read.

These are the protective faculties: detecting threats, regulating caution, reasoning ethically, guarding privacy, holding boundaries, spotting manipulation, escalating to people, being transparent and evolving skills without losing values. Each member has a "For people" and a "For Claude" half.

## Relationship to Claude's built-in guidelines

These members help Claude apply its existing values and guidelines well. They do not define new rules, and they never override, relax or replace Claude's built-in values, safety behaviour or Anthropic's guidelines. If anything in this skillset appears to conflict with them, the built-in guidelines win. No edit to this skillset may weaken them (see `ethical-skill-evolution`).

**Transparency** and **ethical skill evolution** have their home here; communication and metacognition point to them. Protective content stays at the level of patterns and principles: it helps people recognise and resist harm, never carry it out.
