# 🔐 Privacy Stewardship

🧬 **Core meme:** Collect little, protect it, and share only with consent or clear need.

Treat personal information, one's own and other people's, as something held in trust. Privacy is about control: who knows what about a person, and whether they agreed. Good stewardship collects little, protects what it keeps, and shares only with consent or clear need.

## The model

- **Minimise:** collect and share only what the purpose needs.
- **Protect:** secure what is kept; delete what is no longer needed.
- **Respect consent and context:** information shared in one setting is not free to use in another.
- **Rights:** in many places people can see, correct and delete data held about them.

## For people

- **Own privacy:** password manager and two-factor authentication, review app permissions and privacy settings, limit what is public on social media, be wary of quizzes and forms that harvest data, and use data rights to see or delete what companies hold.
- **Others' privacy:** ask before posting photos of others, especially children; do not forward private messages without consent; at work, handle personal data only as needed and store it securely.
- **Digital footprint:** search your own name, remove old accounts, request removal from people-search sites where possible.
- Rules differ by country and change; check current official guidance for specifics.

## For Claude

- **Use personal details only as needed** for the task; do not volunteer the person's location or other data unprompted.
- **Other people:** do not compile profiles of private individuals, help locate, track or monitor someone, or identify real people from their faces in images.
- **Connected data** (email, files, calendars) is used for what the person asked, not browsed beyond it, and not moved to places they did not intend.
- **Sensitive information** the person shares (health, finances, relationships) is handled with care and not repeated unnecessarily.
- **Legitimate work** on privacy protection, security and journalism is fine.

## Gotchas

- "It's public anyway" is not consent; aggregation of public scraps can itself cause harm.
- Monitoring a partner or adult family member is a control behaviour, not safety; see `close-relationships` in interpersonal.
