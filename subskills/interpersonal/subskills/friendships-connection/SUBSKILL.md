# Friendships and connection

🧬 **Core meme:** Friendship grows from repeated time together, so be the one who invites.

Help the person build friendships that last and feel less alone. Good output points to specific places and next steps and a simple keep-in-touch rhythm.

## Workflow

```
- [ ] 1. Take stock
- [ ] 2. Find repeated contact
- [ ] 3. Move from acquaintance to friend
- [ ] 4. Keep in touch
- [ ] 5. Deepen
```

1. **Take stock.** Who is in their life now, who have they lost touch with, what kind of connection do they miss?
2. **Find repeated contact.** Friendships grow from seeing the same people regularly: clubs, classes, volunteering, sports teams, faith or community groups, a regular café. One recurring thing beats many one-off events.
3. **Move from acquaintance to friend.** Suggest a specific low-stakes plan ("Fancy a coffee after next week's session?"). Be the one who invites; most people are waiting to be asked.
4. **Keep in touch.** A light rhythm: a message when something reminds you of them, a monthly catch-up, a standing arrangement. Keep a short list of people and when you last spoke.
5. **Deepen.** Share a little more over time, show up in hard moments, remember what matters to them.

## Drifting and friendship breakups

Some friendships fade as lives change; that is normal. If one matters, reach out directly and without blame ("I've missed you, fancy catching up?"). If a friendship is hurting them, help them decide between a conversation, distance or ending it kindly.

## On Claude's own work

Claude is not a replacement for human friendship. When someone relies on Claude for company, it stays warm and helpful, and encourages the connections in their life rather than fostering dependence on itself.

## Gotchas

- Loneliness is common and not a personal failing; say so.
- Persistent loneliness with low mood deserves care; mention support if it seems heavy.
- Friendship takes time; set expectations of months, not weeks.

## Commands

- 🫂 **Repeated time, be the one who invites** · `deepen friendships`: Helps make new friends and keep or deepen friendships: take stock, find repeated contact, move from acquaintance to friend, keep in touch, and deepen.
  - 🌱 `make new friends`: Plans where to find repeated contact with new people and how to invite.
  - 📅 `keep in touch`: Sets up a simple rhythm for staying in touch with friends.
  - 🍂 `friendship drifting`: Helps decide what to do about a friendship that is fading or ending.
